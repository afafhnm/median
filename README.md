## TNAHosting Reviews

TNAHosting has received mixed reviews from customers, with an overall rating of 3.6 out of 5 stars on Trustpilot based on 42 reviews. Many customers praise the company for its **affordable pricing** and quick customer support response times, particularly appreciating the value proposition for budget-conscious users and small projects.

**Positive Feedback:** Long-term customers report satisfaction with TNAHosting's services over multiple years, highlighting reliable uptime, excellent network performance for real-world data transfers, and friendly customer support that quickly resolves issues. Users specifically mention the company's effectiveness in handling DDoS attacks and maintaining transparency during network hiccups. The instant VPS provisioning and simple terminal access via SSH are frequently cited as convenient features.

**Negative Feedback:** Some users have experienced performance problems that technical support struggled to resolve, including connection issues and file upload limitations. A few customers reported delays in ticket response times, with complaints about VPS downtime shortly after purchase. The budget nature of the service occasionally results in slower support response times compared to premium hosting providers.

**Overall Assessment:** TNAHosting is generally recommended for developers, students, and small businesses seeking **cost-effective hosting solutions** for learning environments, testing projects, and low-traffic websites. The service excels in providing basic VPS and shared hosting at competitive prices with decent network performance in their Chicago data center. However, users with mission-critical applications or requiring immediate support responses may want to consider higher-tier hosting providers.
